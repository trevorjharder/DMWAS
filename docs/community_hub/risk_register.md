## Decisions blocking downstream work

1. Membership status/payment/renewal and expiry semantics, including youth transition at age 19.
2. Source of truth and policy for adult-only eligibility and youth activity participation.
3. Directory opt-in migration/transition and exact field visibility defaults.
4. Activity safety, waivers, organizer responsibilities, locations and minors.
5. Waitlist and cancellation lifecycle.
6. Profile, attendance, message and moderation evidence retention/deletion.
7. Moderation staffing, access and escalation.
8. Staging, migrations, backup/restore, job execution and production approval process.
