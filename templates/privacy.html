Membership

Privacy information

Draft for Society review before public launch.

Information collected

The membership application collects contact details, community of residence, date of birth, mailing address, membership choice, activity and volunteer interests, communication preferences, and (for youth applications) parent or guardian contact and consent information. Website account passwords are stored as password hashes, not as readable passwords.

How information is used

The Society uses application information to review and administer membership, maintain membership and payment records, and send essential membership communications. Newsletters and volunteer-opportunity messages are optional and are sent only when the corresponding preference is selected.

Optional Community Hub profile

Members may create a separate community profile using a chosen display name, biography, general community, interests, and participation goals. Directory participation is off by default and requires an explicit opt-in and administrator approval. Members choose independently whether each optional profile field is visible. Legal name, email, phone, street address, date of birth, and guardian details are not part of the public profile. Profile photos are not currently accepted.

Access, retention, and choices

Membership information is available to authorized Society administrators for membership administration. The Society must approve and publish its retention schedule, privacy contact, and process for changing or withdrawing optional communication preferences before using this draft as its final privacy policy. Members can turn off profile directory participation at any time; profile deletion and broader retention follow the Society's still-to-be-approved retention schedule. Optional communications are separate from essential membership notices.

This notice is not a recreational activity waiver. Any activity-specific waivers or safety acknowledgements must be handled separately.
