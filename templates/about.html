Access to the outdoors in the Mount Waddington region

The District of Mount Waddington Access Society works to improve access to hiking, climbing, and mountaineering opportunities in the region.

View notices Contact the Society

Community

Hiking, climbing, and mountaineering bring people together. We support community climbing facilities and outdoor programs, encourage volunteering, and work to reduce barriers to getting outdoors.

We work with landowners, governments, and local organizations to make outdoor recreation more accessible across the Mount Waddington region.

{{ pillar_photo('mount-st-patrick-hikers', 'A group of hikers looks out from a foggy ridge on Mount St. Patrick') }}

Education

We promote outdoor education and responsible recreation. We share local knowledge of trails, wilderness terrain, and access routes with organizations and authorities that support community safety and emergency preparedness.

We aim to support scholarships, grants, and financial assistance for people pursuing education in outdoor recreation, guiding, tourism, and related fields, especially those who plan to contribute to the region.

{{ pillar_photo('bauza-climbing', 'A roped climber on the rock face at Bauza with a group gathered below') }}

Stewardship

We work to develop, improve, and maintain public access to trails, wilderness routes, and climbing areas. We support infrastructure that helps people reach and use these places safely and responsibly.

{{ pillar_photo('sutton-peak-lookout', 'Two hikers stand on a rocky lookout at Sutton Peak above layers of mountains') }}

Conservation

We promote conservation of natural areas across the Mount Waddington region. We encourage choices that protect the forests, alpine areas, and coastlines people come to explore, from staying on established routes where possible to giving wildlife space.
