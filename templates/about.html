Access to the outdoors in the Mount Waddington region

The District of Mount Waddington Access Society is a society focused on improving access to hiking, climbing, and mountaineering opportunities in the region.

View notices Contact the Society

Our purposes

Supporting responsible outdoor recreation

The Society's constitution describes these purposes:

Access and trails

Promote, develop, improve, and maintain public access to hiking, climbing, and mountaineering opportunities, including trails, wilderness access routes, and climbing areas.

Facilities and programs

Support community climbing facilities, recreational programs, and infrastructure that facilitate safe and responsible outdoor recreation.

Education and stewardship

Promote outdoor education, responsible recreation, conservation, and stewardship of natural areas.

Community participation

Encourage volunteering, reduce barriers to outdoor recreation, and cooperate with governments, landowners, organizations, and other stakeholders.

Local knowledge and safety

Support community safety and emergency preparedness by sharing local knowledge about wilderness terrain, trails, and access routes with appropriate organizations and authorities.

Education support

Support scholarships, grants, and financial assistance for outdoor recreation, guiding, tourism, and related education, with an emphasis on people who intend to contribute to the Mount Waddington region.
