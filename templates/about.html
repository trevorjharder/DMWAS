Access to the outdoors in the Mount Waddington region

The District of Mount Waddington Access Society works to improve access to hiking, climbing, and mountaineering opportunities in the region.

View notices Contact the Society

Community

Everything starts with community. We believe the outdoors has a unique ability to bring people together, create lasting friendships, and foster a sense of belonging.

Our goal is to build an inclusive and welcoming outdoor recreation community across the Mount Waddington region. Through the community climbing wall, organized hikes, group activities, and volunteer opportunities, we aim to connect people of all ages, backgrounds, and experience levels who share a passion for the outdoors.

By building a strong community, we create opportunities for people to learn from one another, share experiences, and discover everything our region has to offer.

Education

A strong community creates opportunities to share knowledge. We believe that everyone should have the chance to develop the skills and confidence needed to safely and responsibly explore the outdoors.

Through mentorship, hands-on learning, and shared experiences, we aim to make outdoor recreation more accessible to newcomers while encouraging experienced members to pass along their knowledge.

Whether someone is learning to climb for the first time, developing backcountry skills, or gaining the confidence to explore independently, education empowers you to take the next step in your outdoor journey.

Conservation

The mountains, forests, rivers, and coastlines of the Mount Waddington region are more than places to recreate—they are ecosystems worth protecting.

We believe that enjoying the outdoors comes with a responsibility to understand and minimize our impact. By promoting environmental awareness, responsible recreation, and respect for the natural world, we hope to inspire a community that values conservation as much as adventure.

Our goal is to ensure that the landscapes we enjoy today remain healthy, accessible, and inspiring for generations to come.

{{ pillar_photo('mount-st-patrick-coast', 'Mist drifts over a forested headland and a rocky shoreline', 480, 1067) }}

Stewardship

A community that understands and values the natural environment is one that is ready to care for it.

Through volunteer trail maintenance, responsible route development, and working alongside First Nations, land managers, and community partners, we aim to make a lasting, positive impact on the places we recreate.

We believe that caring for the land is a shared responsibility. By giving our community opportunities to contribute their time, skills, and knowledge, we can help ensure that outdoor recreation and environmental responsibility go hand in hand.

Together, we build community. Through community, we share knowledge. Through knowledge, we develop respect. And through respect, we take action.
